# Cardinal Comfort Services — Public Signals Brief

Compiled 2026-09-01 by the engagement team from public sources. Quotes are verbatim from the source.

## Company
Cardinal Comfort Services is a residential HVAC and plumbing company headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, operating
six branches across Ohio and Kentucky. In March 2024 Cardinal acquired Cardinal Heating & Air Inc. of Louisville,
Kentucky, which continues to operate under its own name.

leadership."
